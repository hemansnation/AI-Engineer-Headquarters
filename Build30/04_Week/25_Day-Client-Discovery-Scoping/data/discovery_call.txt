Me: Thanks for making time Rahul. Tell me what you want to build.
Rahul: We need an AI chatbot on our website. Our competitor has one and the CEO wants the same.
Me: Understood. Before I think about a chatbot, tell me what is going wrong today that made the CEO ask for it.
Rahul: Customers are angry. They write to support and wait too long for any reply.
Me: How long is too long, in numbers?
Rahul: The first reply takes 3 days on average. We track it in a sheet that the team leads fill every Friday.
Me: How many tickets come in?
Rahul: About 4200 a month. We have 12 agents.
Me: What are people writing about?
Rahul: Mostly two things. Where is my order, and what is the status of my warranty claim. I would say 62 percent of tickets are one of those two.
Me: Walk me through what an agent does with one of those tickets.
Rahul: They open the ticket, copy the order number, search it in our order system, read the status, then type a reply. Warranty ones need them to read our policy PDF first. It takes about 9 minutes each.
Me: What does an agent cost you per hour?
Rahul: About 6 dollars an hour all in.
Me: Where does the order data live?
Rahul: In a MySQL database. Our IT team can give you a read-only login, but it will take about a week to set up.
Me: And the warranty rules?
Rahul: A 14 page PDF. Nobody has updated it in a year, so some rules in it are wrong.
Me: Is there anything about the data I should be careful with?
Rahul: Yes. Tickets have home addresses and phone numbers. Nothing can leave our servers.
Me: If this works, what number changes?
Rahul: First reply under 1 hour for order status questions. That is what I would sign off on.
Me: Who owns this on your side?
Rahul: I do. I run support operations and I can say yes or no.
Me: Do you have a budget in mind?
Rahul: Around 5000 dollars for the first phase, not more.
Me: Last question. Should agents check the answers before customers see them?
Rahul: For now yes. We do not trust it yet, and refunds must never be automatic.
Me: Thank you. I will write this up and send you a scope.
